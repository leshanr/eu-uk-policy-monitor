THE TEST for every item: does a regulatory affairs manager at a mid-sized
manufacturer selling into both markets now have to do something she did not

EVERY "So what" does four things:

  2. States the change         what is permitted now that was not, or the reverse
  3. Gives the date            a deadline or an expiry is the most useful sentence here
  4. Flags the doubt           "the text is ambiguous on X" reads as analysis;
